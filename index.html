Resource
Read "Making Embedded Systems" by Elecia White
Covers the full embedded dev lifecycle — hardware bring-up, firmware architecture, debugging. Much better than random YouTube.
👥 Senior batchmateHigh
Mentor Advice
Stop breadboarding, start soldering your own PCBs
Professor said breadboard projects can't scale past 1st year. Learn KiCad, make real boards — even if they fail.
🧑‍🏫 ECE Lab ProfessorHigh
Course
Complete "Embedded Systems — Shape The World" (UT Austin)
Free on edX. ARM Cortex-M. Builds intuition for memory-mapped I/O, interrupts, RTOS basics.
📚 edX / UT AustinMedium
