About Me

I'm Siril Bhimala — a 2nd Semester ECE AIML student with an obsession for building real physical systems that think.

My track record so far: an RC Plane that flew, a Hexacopter built from scratch, a Maze-Solving Robot, and a 2D game written in C using Raylib. I didn't follow tutorials — I broke things and figured them out.

Right now I'm chasing the intersection of Edge AI + Embedded Systems — running machine learning on microcontrollers, autonomous drones, and eventually Space Tech & Aerospace.

I'm a curious builder. Theory only clicks once I've built something with it. This site is my accountability system — everything I learn, build, and think, logged publicly.

Feedback & Recommendations

4 notes
+ Log a Recommendation
Resource
Read "Making Embedded Systems" by Elecia White
Covers the full embedded dev lifecycle — hardware bring-up, firmware architecture, debugging. Much better than random YouTube for building mental models.
👥Senior batchmate rec High
Mentor Advice
Stop breadboarding, start soldering your own PCBs
Professor said breadboard projects can't scale and won't impress anyone past 1st year. Learn KiCad, make real boards — even if they fail, the process teaches more.
🧑‍🏫ECE Lab Professor High
Course
Complete "Embedded Systems — Shape The World" (UT Austin)
Free on edX. Covers TM4C123 ARM Cortex-M. Builds real intuition for memory-mapped I/O, interrupts, and RTOS basics. Much more structured than docs-only learning.
📚edX / UT Austin Medium
Self Reflection
Do one deep project per semester, not five shallow ones
I noticed I keep starting new projects before finishing old ones. This semester I want to push Hexacopter v2 to GPS hold and TinyML to actual deployment before touching anything new.
